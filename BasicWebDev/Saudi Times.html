Dave's Hot Chicken Opens New Local Spot

A large crowd gathered on the coastal boulevard in Al Khobar today for the official opening of Dave's Hot Chicken.
Local students, families, and food lovers lined up down the sidewalk well before the doors opened,
eager to sample the popular Nashville-style hot chicken chain.

The Menu and Spice Levels

The menu features hot chicken tenders and sliders served with customizable spice levels, ranging from Lite Mild to Reaper.
Customers daring enough to order the Reaper level are required to sign a waiver due to its extreme ghost pepper heat.
Meals are served alongside soft potato buns, kale slaw, pickles, crinkle-cut fries, and signature Dave's sauce.

Community Impact

The launch quickly turned into a lively local gathering. Diners packed the colorful dining room
to challenge their spice tolerance with friends and try out the new menu items.
With a steady crowd lining up throughout the day, the new location appears to be an instant hit for the neighborhood.
